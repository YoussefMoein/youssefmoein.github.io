Forecasting · real data2026

Oil Well Production Forecasting

Monthly oil forecasts for 6,656 Bakken wells, 1, 3 and 6 months ahead, from 105 public regulatory files (404,248 well-months).

Gradient boosting cut error by 21%, 36% and 49% against a "same as last month" forecast at 1, 3 and 6 months ahead, on a held-out 2025–2026 test period.
How I built it
Data
2 million raw rows from the North Dakota DMR, validated for duplicates, invalid dates, negatives and impossible producing days. Every cleaning rule is counted in a report; outliers are flagged, not deleted.
Method
37 leakage-safe features (a unit test proves no future data leaks in), one model per horizon, compared with naive, moving average, decline and Arps baselines. Time-based split plus a 5-window rolling backtest.
Honest limitation
Downtime months are 9.6% of test rows but 17.6% of the error, and the model can't see them coming.

Analytics · AI agent2026

Operations Dashboard with an AI Analytics Agent

A pipeline, star schema and 8-view dashboard for 48 simulated wells, plus an agent that answers plain-language questions with the evidence shown.

The agent uses 14 tools and read-only SQL, and a grounding check throws out any number that isn't in the evidence. 203 automated tests.
How I built it
Pipeline
A deliberately messy extract cleaned into 45,004 well-days with 10 documented rules and a reconciliation report, modelled as a star schema (1 fact, 5 dimensions) and analysed with 14 SQL queries.
Safety
Static SQL validation, a read-only connection, an SQLite authorizer allow-list, and time and row limits. It also says so when a question can't be answered from the data.
Note
All data is simulated; the project demonstrates the method.

Risk modelling2026

Predictive Maintenance for Oil Field Equipment

Ranks 60 simulated pumps, motors and compressors by their risk of failing in the next 72 hours, from a year of hourly sensor readings.

Flagged 31 of 38 failures with a median 50 hours of warning. PR-AUC 0.50 against a 0.022 no-skill level.
How I built it
Method
Leakage-safe, unit-normalised rolling features; logistic regression, random forest and gradient boosting chosen by walk-forward validation with purge gaps.
Checked
Replicated on three freshly simulated fleets never used in development (PR-AUC 0.59–0.62).
Honest limitation
Fewer than half of the strongest flags (46%) were followed by a failure. It's a prioritisation aid, not an alarm.

Anomaly detection · streaming2026

Pipeline Sensor Anomaly Detection

Compared four anomaly detectors on simulated pressure, flow and temperature data, then rebuilt the best as a streaming service with live alerts.

Median 23 ms end-to-end at 200 messages/s with 0 processing errors over 34,558 messages. 126 automated tests.
How I built it
Batch study
Isolation Forest and LOF reached about twice the F1 of a per-sensor statistical baseline and caught 85–93% of simulated events.
Streaming
Producer, message log with offsets and replay, a micro-batched processor, structured alerts, latency monitoring, a live dashboard, and Docker files. Tests enforce that streaming scores match batch scores exactly.
Note
A local simulation on one machine; not connected to any real pipeline.
